# R54 — Source-gated claims and cross-record anti-joins

Project 99aI | 10 November 2026 | New editorial and research evidence checkpoint.

## Research contribution

The selected public documentary comparison separates five evidence objects: (a) acquisition by a named public buyer from an identified registered supplier for a specified AI module and effective period; (b) a legal/contractual or other authority to oversee a particular audit object; (c) dated evidence of the actual exercise of that oversight; (d) an identifiable and attributable vendor response; and (e) a measured endpoint and its causal attribution, if justified.

The contribution is a case-specific, source-aware demonstration of invalid cross-record linkage; it is not a new validated general accountability model, first contract study, prevalence estimate, or causal effect.

## Ten empirical source profiles

1. West Berkshire: its internally created bin-assessment tool is not proof of AI tool purchase merely because a commercial platform was used.
2. Warwickshire: noncommercial model-output provision is not evidence of paid AI procurement.
3. North Northamptonshire: the official £48,000 Magic Notes subscription with Beam Up Ltd establishes acquisition identity, not exercise of its conditional correction clause.
4. North Northamptonshire inspection: a report published after the September 2025 extension describes an assessment dated February–July 2025, not necessarily performance under that extension.
5. Newcastle: ATRS company number 09162820 and related service-award number FC034225 identify different registered companies; a common AWS brand cannot certify exact same contracting entity or AI module terms.
6. Victoria archiving: auditor-reported, statutory-audit-linked supplier retention correction is not a contract cure or AI model performance effect.
7. Victoria software: independent testing and approved rollout is supplier action, not established post-release efficacy.
8. Queensland: documented vendor KPIs and regular reviews; a 98.7% assessment filtering figure is not a measured effect of an identified vendor patch.
9. Kingston: staff reports of 50–60% writing-time savings and a project-reported >96% transcription accuracy metric have different reporting provenance; neither isolates contractual enforcement.
10. Bexley: a different public buyer and trial cannot inherit North Northamptonshire contract terms from a shared product.

## Eleven claim-join examples

One narrowly ADMISSIBLE join: same public buyer, Beam legal company ID, Magic Notes subscription and matching effective dates in the North Northamptonshire notice and instrument.

Ten INADMISSIBLE stronger joins concern acquisition direction, noncommercial supply, unenforced contract text, different audit objects, observation chronology, distinct legal entities, cross-buyer terms, compliance versus model effect, operational filtering versus cure effect, and mixed Kingston outcome reporters. Inadmissibility means that selected public documents do not support that proposition; it never establishes that an unobserved event did not occur.

## Conservation and boundaries

The original register holds 28 event descriptions nested within nine programme families. Original source-coded statuses have no established contractual-entitlement-to-exercise link, two recorded exercise-to-supplier-action relations from noncontractual channels, and no established supplier-action-to-outcome or outcome-to-causal-effect link. These are descriptive counts about a selected register, not a sample prevalence, independent coder reliability, or effect estimate.

Prior studies by Cusenza and Johnson et al., and claims–arguments–evidence assurance methods, already cover procurement and evaluation practices; first-in-field novelty claims are explicitly disallowed. The R51 documentary paper remains frozen at 15 Main plus 11 Appendix pages; R53 hourly scoping screening is separately provisional, with no final PRISMA counts. R54 is a revision-ready text and supplementary source audit, not a complete revised submission manuscript.

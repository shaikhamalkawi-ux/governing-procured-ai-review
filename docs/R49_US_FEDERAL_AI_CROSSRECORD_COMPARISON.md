# R49 cross-record comparison: vendor identity and oversight objects matter

This source-specific, **external context** comparison is not an included new study added to the frozen Scopus/WoS 2,556 identity denominator.

**Award source:** the U.S. government USAspending record for award `70CMSD21P00000127` identifies DHS purchasing **Clearview AI facial-recognition enterprise licenses** from the named Clearview AI supplier, with an award period beginning 3 September 2021. This establishes a **named vendor, purchased facial-recognition license and buyer**. It does not show exercised contract-cure powers, independent technology validation or downstream measured harm reduction. Public award record: https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_70CMSD21P00000127_7012_-NONE-_-NONE- .

**Follow-up institutional audit:** GAO-23-105607 reported use of commercial facial-recognition services within U.S. federal agencies. Its published recommendation follow-up states that Homeland Security Investigations implemented the **Clearview Account Validation and Verification Process in June 2024** to control and annually review account training qualifications. This supports a **buyer-side staff-account training/access control**, not a vendor-issued software patch, legal contract cure or independently attributed performance improvement. The GAO report does not explicitly identify the precise USAspending award ID and cannot be automatically attached to that instrument solely because agency/brand align. Official source: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105607 .

**Non-join counterexample:** separate 2016 Washington Post reporting attributed to a company spokesperson states **Faception** claimed a deal with an unnamed homeland-security agency for highly disputed AI *facial profiling*. Gentzel (2024, BIB00634) repeats an author-level statement that a facial-profiling vendor had a U.S. federal government contract. Faception is **not Clearview AI**; AI facial profiling cannot be substituted for identity matching, and neither source supplies the original Faception signed instrument. The earlier reported contract may have been of a different jurisdiction/entity. Until the exact agency/vendor/product instrument is recovered, BIB00634 remains a **direct candidate only**. Original article DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-024-02156-0 ; secondary article: https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/innovations/wp/2016/05/24/terrorist-or-pedophile-this-start-up-says-it-can-out-secrets-by-analyzing-faces/ .

**Scientific use:** This three-source, bounded comparison illustrates exactly how procurement award and postdeployment agency control can both be documented without proving an exercised supplier remedy or a legally established linkage across source systems. It does not generate a frequency estimate, causal outcome or vendor wrongdoing finding. It strengthens the evidence-role audit but does not rewrite the separate 26-page documentary article.

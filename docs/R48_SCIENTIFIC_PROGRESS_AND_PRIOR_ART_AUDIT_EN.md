## Executive decision

The main problem is not an absence of work on AI public procurement: there are now published analyses of live NHS tender/contract processes, U.S. city procurement and vendor interactions, Canadian municipal outsourcing decisions, and primary research/policy analysis of government AI contract terms. **Do not claim novelty as the first review of public AI procurement, the first government AI contracts study, or the first discussion of vendor accountability.** The defensible review question is which *specific evidence type* each cited source supports: normative proposal, solicitation/vendor selection, executed contract, documented oversight event, vendor response/remedy, and outcome with/without attribution. This is a research-programming hypothesis and an evidence-charting vocabulary, not a proven absence in all other literature.

## Evidence-type ladder (charting protocol, not a scored index)

- **P — Proposal / norm:** recommended accountability clause, framework, procurement advice. No buyer-vendor transaction inferred.
- **S — Sourcing/selection:** dated call for tenders, vendor bid response, evaluation, shortlist, award proposal. A vendor submission does not by itself prove a signed service.
- **C — Contract:** identified public buyer, supplier and actual instrument/version, with identifiable obligations and date. A written audit right does not imply exercise.
- **O — Oversight event:** dated access request, monitoring, audit, incident review or operational performance evaluation connected to the contracted system. Mere stated intention is not execution.
- **R — Response/remedy:** verified supplier response, correction, amendment, remedy, contestation, withholding, penalty or termination with an explicit link to the oversight event. System abandonment at end of grant is not automatically vendor default.
- **X — Outcomes:** documented service/user consequence; distinguish before/after associations from causal designs. Contracted AI accuracy is not itself an oversight success indicator.

These are **nonexclusive evidence roles** and **not an automatically transitive sequence or ordinal score**. A study may support P and S, C and O, etc.; no missing step may be fabricated. Keep **AI used to analyze tender data**, **government in-house AI** and **third-sector R&D** separate from *government procurement of an external AI tool*.

# AlternativeGate03 — independent official-source method gate (11 Nov 2026)

This is a **scientific-method HOLD** report. It does **not** change R55 documentary manuscript findings or R53 systematic-review screening.

## Evidence source and declared frame
Twenty-five previously unused UK public Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (ATRS) disclosures, fixed from official register page-one source positions 31–56 excluding position 53 (earlier source-family overlap). Each is a transparency **record**, not an independently verified executed AI contract. Full official links and concise, original-source-specific paraphrases are archived in the controlled research project; no licensed Scopus/WoS metadata or copyrighted full texts were redistributed.

The 25 self-disclosures report 16 with some third-party role and 9 with no external tool developer declared. Four explicitly say an AI tool was procured; eight describe development or related services without source-verified AI-component awards; four report externally built algorithmic tools whose **disclosed description alone** does not establish ML/AI; nine have no third-party developer declared. AI-platform/API use can occur even when third-party tool-developer flag is 'No'.

Among 300 pairwise **record comparisons**, simple buyer-name similarity at >=0.85 flagged 8 candidate pairs. One pair also shared a disclosed supplier identifier, yet involved distinct published DESNZ tools, so legal tool or contract equivalence cannot be inferred. Zero exact AI-tool-to-contract merges or executed contractual remedies were verified within this pilot.

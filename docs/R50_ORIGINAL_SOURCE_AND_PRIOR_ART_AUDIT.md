## Evidentiary identity controls and originality

**Public buyer, vendor legal entity, product and AI component, contracting period, and source report identity** must be joined on documentary evidence, not a brand name. The source's statement provenance matters: publisher, public-auditor observation, interviewed employee, supplier self-report, signed contract and original outcome measurement are distinct.

Legal **audit entitlement**, dated **exercise**, specific **supplier response**, subsequent **measurement** and credible **attribution** are separate evidence objects, *not* an ordinal score or an automatic six-stage success ladder. Some activities are documented even without any enforceable contractual clause; a valid narrower assertion need not establish every later object.

The closest work already examines actual government AI contracts and disputes (Cusenza), informal procurement channels (CHI), municipal buyer experience (FAccT), vendor declarations (FAccT), and health-service contract execution (NHS). Therefore novelty claims about first vendor-accountability inventory, first AI contract-clause study, first evidence hierarchy, first operational oversight study or first AI procurement review are unjustified. The defensible question is how much each source permits a *particular* post-award oversight and response claim when contract party, authority, action, time, response and effect are not conflated.

Cusenza has **two SSRN identifiers with recorded version relation**, not two independent studies. FAccT 2026 and CHI 2026 Hudig-family studies require report-cohort overlap checks before both are treated as independent corroboration. This release does not silently add any updated-search paper to the original 2,556 denominator.

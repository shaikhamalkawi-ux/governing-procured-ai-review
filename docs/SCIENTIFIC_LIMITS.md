# Scientific and release boundaries — 99aI R41

1. The R38 documentary analysis is retrospective and purposively sampled; it is neither representative nor a completed scoping review.
2. The R39–R41 source-screening effort is independent work toward a scoping review and must not be mixed into the documentary paper as if final.
3. A bibliographic identity is **not** automatically a study; proceedings containers, publication versions and study families require human checks.
4. All 220 title/abstract judgments are AI-assisted **non-final** first-pass labels. No independent human verification is represented.
5. Labels such as `CONTEXT_ONLY`, `AI_USED_IN_PROCUREMENT_ONLY`, or `OUTSIDE_SCOPE_TA` are triage directions, not final PRISMA exclusions.
6. Distinguish AI **purchased for governmental work** from AI used **by a buyer to execute ordinary procurement workflows**.
7. A signed audit right does not show it was exercised. Observed supplier correction does not imply contractual enforcement. A subsequent outcome metric does not prove a causal effect.
8. No pooled effects, prevalence estimates, legal liability findings or universal performance claims can be drawn from 14 selectively investigated examples.
9. Public code can reproduce the integrity of publicly released files but **not** underlying index searches without separately licensed Scopus and Web of Science access.
10. No final PRISMA-ScR flow, verified full-text inclusion count, registration or journal-acceptance claims are authorized by this repository.
11. Dates, source names and registration metadata should be checked against original publisher/government material before a final article version.
12. Human author decisions are needed for author order, licensing of full manuscripts, funding/conflicts, disclosure wording, and persistent DOI.
